## Full description

**Why merchants install PredictaCore**

Shoppers increasingly discover products through AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). Generic product copy leaves money on the table. PredictaCore audits your catalog for AI visibility gaps and fixes them — without guesswork.

**What you get**

- **Free audit** — AI visibility score on your top products (up to 50 best sellers)
- **Preview before apply** — see every change before it goes live
- **One-click Apply** — update titles, descriptions, and structured data
- **Rollback** — restore previous content if needed
- **Organization schema** — theme extension injects brand JSON-LD for AI crawlers
- **Monthly auto-updates** — keeps new products optimized ($15/mo maintenance)

**Pricing**

- Free: audit + preview
- $35 one-time: first month + unlock Apply
- $15/month: automatic catalog refresh (cancel anytime in Shopify Admin)

**Permissions explained**

- Products & content: read/write to optimize catalog copy
- Themes: inject PredictaCore Brand block (schema.org)
- Markets & locales: align SEO with where you actually sell
- Reports: rank products by sales for audit priority

We do **not** access customer PII or order buyer data.
